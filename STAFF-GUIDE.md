# Borizago staff quick guide

This non-technical checklist is for Boriza and future staff members.

## Daily routine

1. Open the website, select **Admin** in the footer and sign in.
2. Check **Notifications** for new bookings and service requests.
3. Review **Bookings** for passenger details, pickup, drop-off and payment.
4. Review **Trips** for the correct date, times, vehicle, capacity and publication status.
5. Use the prepared WhatsApp message when a customer needs an update.

## Prices and locations

Use **Setup** to manage directional Adult, Pensioner, Student and Child prices. Each direction can have a different price. The same screen lets staff add several pickup or drop-off points, choose a default, control their order and disable an old point without changing past bookings.

Amber notices are friendly checks, not errors. They identify prices still using a trip fallback, towns missing a drop-off, or multiple departures on the same route and date. They never delete a trip or block normal work.

## Safe handling

- Confirm a payment only after checking the real payment or proof.
- Compare times and vehicles before changing a possible duplicate trip; two vehicles may be intentional.
- A cancelled booking releases its seat and cannot be reopened.
- Do not share staff passwords, customer ID numbers or admin screenshots publicly.
- Use the dashboard's **Help** tab for the full step-by-step guide.

Customers can use the public [Help & FAQ](https://borizashuttles.pages.dev/help) or contact Boriza directly.
